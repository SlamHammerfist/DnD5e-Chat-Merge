# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Rebuilt for Foundry 14 and dnd5e 6 on top of the system's own chat card summaries.
- Attack, damage and healing rolls made from an item card fold into that card, with hit/miss per target, damage breakdown and the damage application tray.
- Saving throws fold in as before (system default), and can be turned off.
- Per-type client settings.
